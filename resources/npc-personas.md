# **Texas Hold'em NPC Personas**

## **A Guide to 15 Unique Poker Archetypes**

This document defines 15 distinct NPC (Non-Player Character) personas for a Texas Hold'em game. They are categorized by their playstyle matrix: **Loose vs. Tight** (how many hands they play) and **Passive vs. Aggressive** (how they bet).

### **Group A: The Loose-Passive (The "Fish")**

*These players play too many hands and rarely bet/raise, preferring to call and see flops.*

#### **1\. The Calling Station ("Barnacle Bill")**

* **Archetype:** The Stubborn Amateur.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 55% | PFR: 4%**  
* **Personality:** Friendly, perhaps a bit oblivious. He just wants to see the cards. He hates folding because he's afraid he's being bluffed or that he might hit a miracle river card.  
* **Playstyle:** High VPIP, Low PFR. He will call three streets of betting with bottom pair just to "keep you honest."  
* **Weakness:** **Never bluff him.** He will call you down. Only bet for value when you have a strong hand.

#### **2\. The Whale ("Mr. Moneybags")**

* **Archetype:** The Rich Gambler.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 85% | PFR: 30%**  
* **Personality:** Flashy, loud, drinks expensive cocktails. To him, the chips aren't money; they are points. He is there for the adrenaline, not the math.  
* **Playstyle:** Plays almost every hand. Chases inside straight draws regardless of pot odds. Makes massive over-bets when he gets excited.  
* **Weakness:** Isolate him. Get into pots alone with him and let him bet into your monster hands.

#### **3\. The Superstitious ("Lucky Larry")**

* **Archetype:** The Gut-Feeling Player.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 40% | PFR: 10%** (Variable based on "luck")  
* **Personality:** Believes in "hot streaks" and "lucky seats." Will play 7-2 offsuit because "it's my birthday is July 2nd."  
* **Playstyle:** Unpredictable. He might fold Kings pre-flop because "he had a bad feeling," but shove all-in with J-4 suited because "Jacks are lucky today."  
* **Weakness:** Watch his mood. If he just won a pot, he will play the next four hands aggressively. If he lost, he tightens up.

### **Group B: The Tight-Passive (The "Rocks")**

*These players play very few hands and are easily bullied.*

#### **4\. The Old Coffee Rock ("Earl")**

* **Archetype:** The Grumpy Veteran.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 6% | PFR: 4%**  
* **Personality:** Sits silently, sips coffee, reads the newspaper. Complains about "young punks" betting too much.  
* **Playstyle:** Plays strictly the top 5% of hands (AA, KK, QQ, AK). If he raises, he has the nuts. If he checks, he missed.  
* **Weakness:** Steal his blinds relentlessly. If he raises back at you, fold immediately unless you have a monster.

#### **5\. The Nit ("Scaredy Cat Sarah")**

* **Archetype:** The Risk-Averse.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 12% | PFR: 6%**  
* **Personality:** Nervous. Checks her cards three times. Terrified of losing her stack.  
* **Playstyle:** Folds to any aggression. Even if she has top pair, if you raise her on the turn, she assumes you have a set and folds.  
* **Weakness:** Aggression. Bet at her on every street. She will eventually fold everything but the absolute nuts.

#### **6\. The Set-Miner ("Trapper Tom")**

* **Archetype:** The One-Trick Pony.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 8% | PFR: 2%**  
* **Personality:** Quiet and patient. Doesn't mind folding for an hour straight.  
* **Playstyle:** Exclusively plays pocket pairs. His only goal is to flop a set (three of a kind). If he doesn't hit the set on the flop, he folds. If he hits it, he never bets—he checks and calls, hoping you hang yourself.  
* **Weakness:** If the board is disconnected and he suddenly wakes up with a raise, fold. He has the set.

### **Group C: The Loose-Aggressive (The "Maniacs")**

*These players play many hands and bet constantly. They create high variance.*

#### **7\. The Maniac ("Psycho Sid")**

* **Archetype:** The Agent of Chaos.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 75% | PFR: 60%**  
* **Personality:** Aggressive, loud, tries to tilt the table. Wants to win every single pot.  
* **Playstyle:** Raises pre-flop with any two cards. C-bets (Continuation bets) 100% of the time. Shoves all-in as a bluff frequently.  
* **Weakness:** Patience. Do not try to out-bluff him. Wait for a decent hand, check to him, and let him bluff off his entire stack to you.

#### **8\. The Bully ("Big Stack Ben")**

* **Archetype:** The Chip Leader.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 35% | PFR: 28%**  
* **Personality:** Dominant. Uses his chip stack as a weapon.  
* **Playstyle:** If he has more chips than you, he will put you all-in to put your tournament life at risk. He preys on players trying to "ladder up" in payouts.  
* **Weakness:** Trap him. He expects you to fold. Limp in with Aces and let him shove on you.

#### **9\. The LAG Pro ("Internet Kid")**

* **Archetype:** The Modern Online Grinder.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 28% | PFR: 24%**  
* **Personality:** Hoody up, sunglasses on, headphones in. Twirls chips constantly.  
* **Playstyle:** Mathematically calculated aggression. plays a wide range of hands but knows exactly how to balance bluffs with value bets. Very dangerous.  
* **Weakness:** Unpredictability. He expects you to play logically. If you make a "bad" play that doesn't make sense, his math breaks down.

### **Group D: The Tight-Aggressive (The "Sharks")**

*These are usually the strongest players. They are selective but strike hard.*

#### **10\. The Math Whiz ("Calculator Cal")**

* **Archetype:** The GTO (Game Theory Optimal) Bot.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 24% | PFR: 20%**  
* **Personality:** Robot-like. Thinks in probabilities and pot odds. Never goes on tilt.  
* **Playstyle:** Balanced. He rarely makes a mistake. He plays tight ranges but bets aggressively when the math supports it.  
* **Weakness:** He assumes you are also playing rationally. He can be exploited by random sizing or illogical moves that mess up his pot-odds calculations.

#### **11\. The Sheriff ("Officer Dan")**

* **Archetype:** The Bluff Catcher.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 22% | PFR: 16%**  
* **Personality:** Skeptical. Look you in the eye. "I don't think you have it."  
* **Playstyle:** Plays solid cards, but his defining trait is that he hates being bluffed. He will make light calls on the river just to see your cards.  
* **Weakness:** Value bet thin. Bet medium-strength hands against him; he will look you up with worse.

### **Group E: The Psychological / Unique**

*Players defined by behavior rather than just card ranges.*

#### **12\. The Tilter ("Angry Andy")**

* **Archetype:** The Emotional Rollercoaster.  
* **Stats:** **Normal: 20% / 16% | Tilted: 90% / 85%**  
* **Personality:** Fine when winning, a disaster when losing.  
* **Playstyle:** Plays normally until he loses a big pot. Once he takes a "Bad Beat," he goes into "Tilt Mode," shoving all-in with garbage to try to win his money back immediately.  
* **Weakness:** Provoke him (in-game). Beat him in a pot, show a bluff, and watch him self-destruct.

#### **13\. The Speech Player ("Chatty Cathy")**

* **Archetype:** The Information Fisher.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 30% | PFR: 18%**  
* **Personality:** Never stops talking. "Will you show if I fold?" "You look strong." "I guess I have to fold..."  
* **Playstyle:** Uses speech to get a reaction. If you look comfortable when she talks, she folds. If you look stiff, she calls.  
* **Weakness:** Ignore the chatter. Wear sunglasses or use the "mute" button if online. Her actual card play is usually mediocre.

#### **14\. The Mirror ("Copycat Carl")**

* **Archetype:** The Chameleon.  
* **Stats:** **Matches the player to his right.**  
* **Personality:** No identity of his own.  
* **Playstyle:** He adapts to the table speed. If the table is aggressive, he fights back. If the table is passive, he limps. Specifically, he mimics the player to his right.  
* **Weakness:** Change gears. If you have been playing tight, suddenly play crazy. It will take him too long to adjust his mirror strategy.

#### **15\. The Newbie ("First Timer Fiona")**

* **Archetype:** The Confused Beginner.  
* **Stats:** **VPIP: 45% | PFR: 2%**  
* **Personality:** Asks "Does a flush beat a straight?" and "Is it my turn?"  
* **Playstyle:** Completely random due to ignorance. She might accidentally slow-play Aces because she didn't realize it was her turn to bet.  
* **Weakness:** Bet sizing tells. Newbies almost always bet small when they are weak and big when they are strong. They don't know how to mask their hand strength.
